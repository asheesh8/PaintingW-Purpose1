Library & Community Hall

Bright walls, clean lines against exposed timber, and an open stair finished to gallery standard.

Public buildings need durable finishes and a crew that works around people. We sequenced rooms so the building could stay in use, and kept the timber ceilings and stone untouched.

Walls and ceilings were finished in a scrubbable low-sheen white that makes the wood and daylight do the work.
