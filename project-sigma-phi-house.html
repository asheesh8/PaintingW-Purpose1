Exterior · Columns, trim and entry

Sigma Phi House

Four Corinthian columns, a full pediment and a fanlight entry, made crisp again against red brick.

Classical trim shows everything. Capitals, dentils and the fanlight over the door are slow work, done by hand from the right equipment.

We worked around a busy house, protected the steps and plantings, and left the entry looking the way it was built to look.
