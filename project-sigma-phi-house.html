Institutional · Columns, trim and entry

Sigma Phi House

Four Corinthian columns, a full pediment and a fanlight entry, restored to read crisp against red brick.

Classical trim is unforgiving. Capitals, dentils and the fanlight over the door all show every drip, so the work is slow, detailed and done from the right equipment.

We scheduled around a busy house, protected the marble steps and landscaping, and left the entry looking the way it was built to look.
