Purpose

We paint to help end homelessness in Vermont.

Homelessness affects our whole community, and the solutions are few and far between.

Part of what every client pays goes to CVOEO, the Champlain Valley Office of Economic Opportunity, and straight to helping people find a long-term, stable home. So far that is more than $15,000 and twelve people helped into secure housing, with the Joy & Jerry Meyers Cup along the way. It adds nothing to your price.

Brothers Gabe and Zach Dunn started Painting With Purpose to prove a painting company could be really good at the work and really good for the community. One of them is on nearly every job, and Gabe takes most of the photographs on this site.
