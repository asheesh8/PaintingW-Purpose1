Our purpose

We paint to help end homelessness in Vermont.

Painting With Purpose isn’t just a name, and we don’t just paint.

Why

Homelessness affects our whole community, and solutions are few and far between. Our charitable contributions to CVOEO, the Champlain Valley Office of Economic Opportunity, go directly to helping people who don’t have a long-term, stable home.

Every project we take on is part of that. When you hire a Vermont crew that does precise, careful work, part of what you pay goes back into the community you live in.
