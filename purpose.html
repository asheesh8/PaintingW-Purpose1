Purpose

We paint to help end homelessness in Vermont.

Homelessness touches the whole community, and the answers are few. We wanted our work to be one of them.

Part of the profit from every job goes to CVOEO, the Champlain Valley Office of Economic Opportunity. It helps people in shelters move into permanent housing by covering security deposits, a first month’s rent and utilities. So far that is more than $15,000, and more than a dozen people in secure homes. It adds nothing to your price.

Zach and Gabe Dunn started Painting With Purpose in 2022 to show that a painting company could be very good at the work and good for the place it works. The business plan took first place at the Joy and Jerry Meyers Cup at UVM. More than a hundred jobs later, the idea is the same.
