Vermont painters working to end homelessness.

Painting With Purpose is a Greater Burlington crew of painters who care about the details: protected floors and plants, prep done right, and a site cleaned every evening. Brothers Gabe and Zach Dunn started the company to prove a painting business could be really good at the work and really good for the community. One of them is on nearly every job, and Gabe takes most of the photographs on this site.

Community Engagement

Homelessness affects our whole community, and the solutions are few and far between. Part of what every client pays goes to CVOEO, the Champlain Valley Office of Economic Opportunity, and straight to helping people find a long-term, stable home.

So far that is more than $15,000, twelve people helped into secure housing, and the Joy & Jerry Meyers Cup along the way.

We value quality you can see, trust and respect for your home, a clean worksite and clear communication. You always know what’s next and when.
