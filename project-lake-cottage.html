Lake Cottage

Lake Champlain

A lakeside camp in a deep lake blue, with white rails and a screened porch ready for another fifty summers.

01Lake houses take a beating from sun, wind and water. We stripped back failing paint, replaced and primed soft boards, and chose a coating built for exposure off the lake.

02The result is a cottage that looks freshly built from the water and from the road, with rails and porch details picked out in white.
