Lake Cottage

A lakeside camp in deep lake blue, with white rails and a screened porch ready for many more summers.

Houses on the lake take sun, wind and water from every side. Failing paint came off, soft boards were replaced, and everything was primed before color went on.

Rails and porch details are picked out in white, so the cottage reads clearly from the water and the road.
