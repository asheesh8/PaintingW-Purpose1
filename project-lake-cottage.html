Lake Cottage

A lakeside camp in a deep lake blue, with white rails and a screened porch ready for another fifty summers.

Location
Lake Champlain
Type
Residential, exterior
Surfaces
Clapboard, porch, rails, deck
Prep
Wash, scrape, board repair, prime
Finish
Satin body, gloss rails
Colors on this job
Lake blue#4F6F93
White trim#F1F1EE
Metal roof#A9AEB0
Deck grey#8A8C86

Lake houses take a beating from sun, wind and water. We stripped back failing paint, replaced and primed soft boards, and chose a coating built for exposure off the lake.

The result is a cottage that looks freshly built from the water and from the road, with rails and porch details picked out in white.
