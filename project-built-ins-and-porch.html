Built-ins & Porch

Small spaces, high detail: a slate built-in with matching wainscot, and a porch floor painted in a diamond pattern.

Location
Greater Burlington
Type
Residential, interior and porch
Surfaces
Cabinetry, wainscot, porch floor, ceiling
Prep

Finish

Colors on this job

Wall white#F1F0EC
Porch grey#9EA3A2

Built-ins are furniture. They get sanded between coats, sprayed or laid off with a fine brush, and cured properly before anything goes back on the shelves.

Outside, the porch floor got a hand-laid diamond pattern in two greys under a bright white beadboard ceiling.
