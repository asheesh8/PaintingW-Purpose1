Residential · Cabinetry and porch floor

Built-ins & Porch

A slate built-in with matching wainscot, and a porch floor laid out in a two-grey diamond.

Built-ins are furniture. They are sanded between coats, finished smooth and left to cure before anything goes back on the shelves.

Outside, the porch floor was taped and painted in a diamond pattern under a bright white beadboard ceiling.
