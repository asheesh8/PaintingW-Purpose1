Built-ins & Porch

About the job

Built-ins are furniture. They get sanded between coats, sprayed or laid off with a fine brush, and cured properly before anything goes back on the shelves.

Outside, the porch floor got a hand-laid diamond pattern in two greys under a bright white beadboard ceiling.
