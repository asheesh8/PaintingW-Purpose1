How a job runs

  1. Consultation

    We visit, measure and talk through colors, surfaces and repairs.

  2. Written proposal

    Scope, schedule and price, in writing.

  3. Prep and paint

    Protection first, then prep, then paint. The site is cleaned at the end of every day.

  4. Final walkthrough

    We always inspect our work to make sure our standards and yours are aligned and met.

Where we work

All of Vermont, and a little past its borders.

Based in Winooski, we paint across Vermont, in Clinton, Essex, Warren and Washington counties in New York, and in Grafton, Sullivan and Cheshire counties in New Hampshire. Just outside the haze? Ask us anyway.
