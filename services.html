Services

Five services, one standard.

Qualified, vetted painters. A clean worksite every evening. Clear communication from the first call to the final walkthrough.

Service 01

Residential painting

Interiors and exteriors, from a single bedroom to a full Victorian restoration. Cabinets, trim, siding, porches and the repairs underneath them.

  • Interior walls & ceilings
  • Exterior siding & trim
  • Cabinets & built-ins
  • Drywall & plaster repair
Service 02

Commercial painting

Storefronts, offices, multi-unit and public buildings. We work in phases and off-hours so your doors stay open.

  • Offices & retail
  • Multi-unit residential
  • Public & institutional
  • After-hours scheduling
Service 03

Deck staining & maintenance

Cleaning, brightening, stripping where needed, and staining with products made for Vermont winters. Ask about a yearly maintenance visit.

  • Wash & brighten
  • Strip failing finish
  • Board-by-board staining
  • Rail & stair detail
Service 04

EMP lead inspections

Certified Essential Maintenance Practices inspections for pre-1978 homes and rental properties, plus lead-safe repair and abatement when it’s needed.

  • Certified EMP inspections
  • Pre-1978 homes & rentals
  • Lead-safe work practices
  • Abatement & repair
Service 05

Gutter cleaning

Fall and spring cleanouts that keep water off your siding, away from your foundation, and off fresh paint.

  • Fall & spring cleanouts
  • Downspout flushing
  • Minor repairs
  • Pairs with exterior painting

How a job runs

Four steps
  1. Step 01

    Walkthrough

    We visit, measure, talk through colors and surfaces, and note any repairs.

  2. Step 02

    Written estimate

    An itemized proposal with scope, products, schedule and price. No surprises.

  3. Step 03

    Prep & paint

    Protection first, then careful prep, then paint. The site is cleaned every evening.

  4. Step 04

    Final walkthrough

    We review every surface together and touch up anything before we call it done.
