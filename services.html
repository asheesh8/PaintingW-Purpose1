Services

Five services, one standard.

  • 01

    Residential painting

    Interiors and exteriors, from a single room to a full Victorian restoration: cabinets, trim, siding, porches and the repairs underneath them.

  • 02

    Commercial painting

    Storefronts, offices, multi-unit and public buildings, worked in phases and off-hours so your doors stay open.

  • 03

    Decks & porches

    Cleaning, brightening, stripping where needed, and staining with products made for Vermont winters.

  • 04

    Lead-safe work

    Certified Essential Maintenance Practices inspections for pre-1978 homes and rentals, with lead-safe repair when it’s needed.

  • 05

    Gutter cleaning

    Fall and spring cleanouts that keep water off your siding, away from your foundation, and off fresh paint.

How a job runs

  1. Walkthrough

    We visit, measure, talk through colors and surfaces, and note any repairs.

  2. Written estimate

    An itemized proposal with scope, products, schedule and price.

  3. Prep and paint

    Protection first, then careful prep, then paint. The site is cleaned every evening.

  4. Final walkthrough

    We review every surface together and touch up anything before we call it done.
