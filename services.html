Services

Five services, one standard.

  • 01

    Residential painting

    Interiors and exteriors, from one room to a whole house. Siding, trim, doors, cabinets and porches, with rot repair when the wood needs it first.

  • 02

    Commercial painting

    Offices, storefronts, multi-unit and public buildings, inside and out. Phased around your hours, with crews experienced on lifts.

  • 03

    Decks & porches

    Washing, sanding, repair and stain or paint for wood, composite and metal decks.

  • 04

    EMP lead inspections

    Essential Maintenance Practices inspections for owners and property managers. We are EPA lead certified, so older homes are handled safely from the first scrape.

  • 05

    Gutter cleaning

    Spring and fall cleanouts, so water keeps moving through a Vermont winter.

How a job runs

  1. Walkthrough

    We visit, measure and talk through colors, surfaces and repairs.

  2. Written estimate

    Scope, schedule and price, in writing and free.

  3. Prep and paint

    Protection first, then prep, then paint. The site is cleaned at the end of every day.

  4. Final walkthrough

    We go over the finished job with you before we call it done.
