Why choose Painting With Purpose?

Finding an available painting company that lives up to your high quality standards can be difficult.

You want experienced painters, budget-friendly pricing and top quality results. Painting With Purpose is here to fill all of your needs.

Everybody on our planet seeks a purpose. Here at Painting With Purpose, there’s purpose behind every brush stroke. We design intentional scopes of work and perform every task, from beginning to end, at the highest level.

1% of your base contract value goes towards the charity of your choosing after a successful closing. Painting With Purpose believes in enriching the community it has sprouted and grown from. We pledge to stick to our Vermont roots, where people help people.

Be a part of continued mission success by hiring the painters with a purpose.
