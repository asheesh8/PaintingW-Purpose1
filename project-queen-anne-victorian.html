Queen Anne Victorian

Greater Burlington

Layers of spindles, brackets and fish-scale shingle, each one scraped, primed and cut in by hand.

01Queen Anne houses reward patience. Every bracket, baluster and band of trim is its own small project, and the paint has to sit cleanly on wood that has seen a century of Vermont winters.

02The crew worked lead-safe from the first scrape, kept the porch usable for the family throughout, and finished with a layered scheme that reads cleanly from the street and up close.
