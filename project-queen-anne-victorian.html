Queen Anne Victorian

Spindles, brackets and fish-scale shingle, each one scraped, primed and cut in by hand.

A Queen Anne rewards patience. Every bracket and baluster is its own small job, on wood that has seen a century of Vermont winters.

The crew worked lead-safe from the first scrape and kept the porch open for the family throughout.
