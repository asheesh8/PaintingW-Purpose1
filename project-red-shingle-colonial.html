Red Shingle Colonial

Cedar shingle in barn red, black shutters and white trim.

Shingle drinks paint. Even color across thousands of edges means brushing every course and keeping a wet edge the whole way across a wall.

Shutters came down, the trim went back to bright white, and the house kept its New England face.
