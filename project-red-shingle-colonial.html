Red Shingle Colonial

Cedar shingle in barn red, black shutters and white trim, cut in one window at a time.

Location
Chittenden County
Type

Surfaces

Colors on this job

White trim#F2F1EC

Shingle siding drinks paint. Getting even color across thousands of edges means back-brushing every course and keeping a wet line all the way across a wall.

Shutters came down, trim went back to bright white, and the house kept its classic New England face.
